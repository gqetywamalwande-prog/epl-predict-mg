How this works

The model studies every match from last season to estimate how many goals each team scores and concedes, plus a boost for playing at home. Newly promoted teams get the average strength of last season's promoted teams.

For each fixture it turns those strengths into expected goals, then adds up the chance of every possible scoreline to get the win, draw and loss probabilities.

The projected table adds the points teams have already won to the expected points (3 × chance of a win + 1 × chance of a draw) from the matches still to play.
